Windage is 1/4 MOA clicks, 15 MOA per rotation, 45 MOA total — dial it or hold it. Dial the same side the wind comes from. Default is 10 mph full value so you can scale it in your head: double for 20, halve for a quartering wind.

Truing

Shoot at a known distance — 600 or beyond is where it actually bites. Enter the elevation that put rounds on target, and the solver back-solves muzzle velocity.

Range-day conditions are stored with the result. The solver fits whatever velocity makes the impact work — if the pressure you entered was wrong, that error rides along in the "trued" number forever unless you can see what it was fit against.
